Variety Of Classes Offered

  • Vinyasa & Flow (Dynamic Movement)
    • Rise & Shine Vinyasa: A morning practice combining smooth movements and deep breaths to naturally wake up your body and sharpen your focus.
    • Ignite: Core & Flow: A fast-paced, uplifting class focusing on building full-body core strength, improving balance, and cultivating physical stamina.
    • Weekend Community Flow: An accessible, playful, all-levels class designed to celebrate collective movement and connect with fellow students.
  • Hatha & Alignment (Foundations & Pace)
    • Gentle Hatha Yoga: A slower, intentional practice that breaks down foundational alignments, making it perfect for total beginners or anyone wanting a mindful, steady pace.
  • Restorative & Yin (Deep Relaxation)
    • The Reset: Deep Stretch: A quiet, low-intensity class where you hold gentle floor poses supported by props to melt away tight muscle tissue and calm a busy nervous system.
