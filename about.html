Meet Our Instructors

David Vance (Yin & Hatha Specialist)

David believes yoga is for every single body, no matter your age or flexibility. With a background in somatic movement and mindfulness coaching, his classes focus on releasing deep joint tension and calming the nervous system. David guides students with a quiet, patient voice. He uses props generously and encourages everyone to honor where their body is today.

Maya Lin (Lead Vinyasa Instructor)

Maya brings warmth, steady energy, and creative sequencing to every class. She completed her 500-hour training in Costa Rica and focuses on linking mindful movement with deep, rhythmic breathing. Her classes challenge your physical edge while keeping the atmosphere light and supportive. Expect a dynamic flow, great music, and a safe space to try something new.

Elena Rostova (All-Levels Instructor)

Elena discovered yoga over a decade ago as a way to manage stress from a fast-paced corporate job. Now, she loves helping others find that same sense of grounded relief on the mat. Her teaching style blends alignment-focused alignment with an accessible, friendly approach. Elena wants every student to walk out the door feeling stronger, taller, and a little more at peace.

Studio Accessibility Features

  • Physical Space

    • Accessible ramps, wide doorways, a zero-step main entry, and a fully ADA-compliant bathroom.

  • Prop Support

    • Complimentary access to visual and physical modifications, including height-adjustable chairs, extra-firm blocks, yoga wheel props, and support straps.

  • Class Modifications

    • Trained instructors who offer explicit verbal cues for variations, seated options, and trauma-informed adjustments.

  • Sensory Choices

    • Soft, dimmable lighting, fragrance-free spaces, and designated quiet areas to support neurodivergent or sensory-sensitive practitioners.

    • Studio Policies and Guidelines

    • Booking & Attendance

      • Pre-Registration Required

        • - We highly recommend booking your class online or via our app ahead of time to secure your mat space.

      • Late Arrival Policy

        • - Classes start exactly on time.

        - For safety and to prevent disruption, the studio doors lock when class begins.

        - Late arrivals will not be permitted to enter or practice.

      • Cancellation Policy

        • - You must cancel your reservation at least 2 hours before class starts.

        - Failure to do so will result in a lost class credit (for class pack holders) or a $10 late-cancel fee (for unlimited members).

  • Studio Etiquette

    • Device-Free Zone

      • - All cell phones, smartwatches, and electronics must be completely silenced and left in the designated locker/storage cubbies.

      - No phones are permitted on the practice floor.

    • Fragrance & Scents

      • - Out of respect for students with allergies and respiratory sensitivities, please refrain from wearing heavy perfumes, colognes, or strongly scented body lotions.

    • Footwear

      • - Remove your shoes immediately upon entering the studio lobby.

      - Shoes are not permitted inside the practice rooms.

  • Safety & Hygiene

    • Clean Gear

      • - Students are welcome to bring their own mats or use studio-provided mats.

      - All studio props and mats must be thoroughly wiped down with our plant-based disinfectant sprays after use.

    • Health & Injuries

      • - Please inform your instructor before class starts if you are pregnant, managing an injury, or have any medical conditions so they can offer safe modifications.

  • Memberships & Purchases

    • Refund Policy

      • - All sales are final.

      - Class packages, memberships, workshops, and retail items are non-refundable and non-transferable.

    • Membership Pauses

      • - Monthly unlimited auto-renew members can pause their membership once per calendar year for up to 30 days.

      - Pause requests must be submitted via email at least 7 days before your next billing cycle.
