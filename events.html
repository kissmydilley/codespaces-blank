RIVERBEND YOGA STUDIO

Monthly Special Events

  • Quarter 1: Intention & Grounding

    • January: The Art of Sankalpa (Intention Setting & Yoga Nidra)

      • - Workshop replacing stressful New Year's resolutions with deep relaxation and mindful intention-setting.

    • February: Heart-Opening Flow & Cacao Ceremony

      • - Community-focused, gentle back-bending practice to increase thoracic mobility and cultivate self-compassion.

    • March: Spring Equinox Detox & Twist Intensive

      • - Dynamic Vinyasa workshop focusing on twisting postures and breathwork to welcome the changing season and build heat.

  • Quarter 2: Growth & Alignment

    • April: Foundations of Flight (Arm Balances for Beginners)

      • - Workshop breaking down core engagement, wrist prep, and weight distribution to confidently try poses like Crow and Side Plank.

    • May: Stress Reset (Myofascial Release & Deep Stretch)

      • - Therapeutic class using tennis or massage balls to target tight fascia, relieve chronic tension, and open up the hips and shoulders.

    • June: Summer Solstice 108 Sun Salutations

      • - Powerful, meditative, community-driven event to mark the longest day of the year through repetitive movement.

  • Quarter 3: Exploration & Play

    • July: Outdoor Park Flow & Sound Bath

      • - Connection-centered event combining an accessible outdoor morning flow with a deeply relaxing crystal singing bowl sound bath.

    • August: Unshakable Balance (Inversions & Core Stability)

      • - Playful session focusing on headstands, forearm stands, or wall-supported handstands to build full-body strength and overcome fear.

    • September: Autumn Equinox Slow Down (Yin & Journaling)

      • - Calming workshop designed to slow down the nervous system through longer-held floor postures, paired with reflective journaling.

  • Quarter 4: Restoration & Reflection

    • October: Breath as Medicine (Pranayama & Meditation Masterclass)

      • - Deep dive into traditional yogic breathing techniques to manage anxiety, increase lung capacity, and sharpen mental clarity.

    • November: Radical Rest (Trauma-Informed Restorative Yoga)

      • - Deeply comforting, quiet workshop using maximum props to completely support the physical body during a stressful holiday season.

    • December: Winter Solstice Candlelight Renewal

      • - Quiet, cozy evening of candlelit gentle movement, live soft music or sound bowls, and guided meditation to honor the shortest day of the year.
